The Rules

How to Play — 3 Simple Steps

1

The Flavour Warm‑Up

Take a tiny nibble of each cheese and dip. Taste them all individually. Soak in the flavours and awaken the chef in you — get a feel for what pairs sweet, what pairs bold, and what surprises you.

2

Invent Your Custom Bite

Take one cracker or crispbread slice and build your master entry. Layer it up with an imaginative combination:

3

Taste‑Test & Vote

Give your snack entry a creative name. Replicate it so every player gets exactly one single-bite sample. Taste together and score each entry on the ballot below — then tally the totals and crown your champions!

💡

Pro Taste Tip from The Pune Platter Co.

Artisanal gourmet cheese tastes best when it is not freezing cold! Let the platter rest outside your refrigerator for 15–20 minutes before playing. This releases the rich, buttery aromas and makes the textures beautifully soft and satisfying.

The Official Voting Matrix
