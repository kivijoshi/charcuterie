The Rules

How to Play — 3 Simple Steps

1

Call Your Lucky Code

On your turn, close your eyes and call out any 3‑digit number where each digit is between 1 and 4. For example: 4 – 2 – 1.

Each digit maps to one item in each category of your Flavor Key.

2

Decode Your Snack

Match your digits to the Flavor Key you set up. Code 4–2–1 means:

  • Cheese #4
  • +
  • Spread #2
  • +
  • Crunch #1

Build it on one cracker and replicate for each player.

3

Taste, Rate & Vote

Everyone gets one single-bite sample. Rate the lucky creation from 1 (flavor nightmare) to 5 (absolute fortune). Record scores in the matrix and tally the totals!

🎲

Folded Chits Alternative

To make it fully blind, write numbers 1–4 on separate paper chits and place them into 3 bowls (one bowl per category). Each player draws one chit from each bowl on their turn to seal their flavour fate!

💡

Pro Taste Tip from The Pune Platter Co.

Artisanal gourmet cheese tastes best when it is not freezing cold! Let the platter rest outside your refrigerator for 15–20 minutes before playing. This releases the rich, buttery aromas and makes the textures beautifully soft and satisfying.
