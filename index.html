Meet Manasi

A Decade in Europe, a Passion Brought Home

I am Manasi — a hotel management graduate with an obsession for great cheese and charcuterie that only deepened after ten years of living in Germany. Surrounded by world-class artisanal produce, weekend markets, and the storied cheese culture of neighbouring Netherlands, I went from enthusiast to expert one tasting at a time.

When I came back to Pune, I realised there was nowhere I could find a platter that felt truly thoughtful — the kind you stumble upon in a little shop in Cologne or Amsterdam. So I decided to build one myself, curating every board with the same care and knowledge I picked up across Europe, and delivering it right to your door.

Every platter from The Pune Platter Co. is a piece of that journey — made for people who appreciate the real thing.
